Box Model Exploration

Understanding content, padding, border, and margin — and the impact of box-sizing.

Default: content-box

In content-box, width and height only include the content. Padding and border are added outside, making the box appear larger.

Modified: border-box

In border-box, width and height include content, padding, and border — keeping the box’s total size consistent and easier to control.
