Welcome to Jaipur

Jaipur, the capital city of Rajasthan, is known for its royal heritage, majestic forts, and colorful bazaars. The city is often called the “Pink City” because of the pink-colored buildings in its old town area.

Rich Culture & Heritage

Jaipur is a treasure of traditions and art. From folk dances and music to colorful festivals like Teej and Gangaur, the city celebrates its culture with unmatched enthusiasm and beauty.

Delicious Rajasthani Cuisine

Jaipur offers mouth-watering delicacies like Dal Baati Churma, Ghewar, and Laal Maas. The flavors of Rajasthan are a perfect blend of spice, sweetness, and aroma that captivate every food lover.

Famous Attractions

Some of the must-visit places in Jaipur include the Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar, and Amber Fort. Each monument tells a story of grandeur and royal legacy.
