The Beauty of Typography

Typography is not just about choosing fonts — it’s about creating readable, balanced, and visually appealing content. The first impression of any webpage often comes from its typography choices.

Designers often combine multiple font families to establish hierarchy. For example, serif fonts like Georgia or Times New Roman add elegance, while sans-serif fonts like Arial or Helvetica bring a modern and clean appearance.

Even monospace fonts have their charm — used mostly for code or technical text, they provide structure and precision. Good typography uses line-height and letter-spacing to make reading comfortable and easy on the eyes.
